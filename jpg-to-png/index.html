Frequently asked questions

Does converting JPG to PNG improve quality?

No. PNG keeps the image exactly as it is now, but it can't bring back detail that JPG compression already removed.

Are my images uploaded anywhere?

No. Pixelsmith processes every image inside your browser using your own device, so your photos are never sent to a server.

Is Pixelsmith free?

Yes. There's no sign-up, no watermark, and no limit on how many images you can process.

Can I edit many images at once?

Yes. Add as many images as you like, edit one, click "Apply these settings to all images", and download them all as a single ZIP file.
